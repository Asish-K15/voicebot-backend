Aliases: how to get admission; admission procedure; enrollment process; apply to CUTM; Centurion University admission process

Important Information:
  - The application fee for CUEE-2026 is Rs 299 (limited offer, originally Rs 600).
  - Exam dates are communicated via email and SMS one week before the test.
  - Applicants should monitor their registered email and phone for all communication.
  - For offline admission assistance, call 8260077222.
  - The cancellation and refund policy follows UGC guidelines for the same academic year.

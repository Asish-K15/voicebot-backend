What is the Career Development Cell?
Overview: CUTM has a Career Development Cell (CDC) that provides placement training, company engagement, and recruitment support for students across all programmes.
Details:
  - The Career Development Cell provides a platform for collaboration between students and companies.
  - Activities include career guidance, employability and aptitude training, communication skills, resume support, mock interviews, employer engagement, internship support, and placement drives.
  - Placement statistics for the 2025 graduate batch: Highest CTC 16 LPA, Average CTC 4.65 lakhs, 39 students with CTC more than 7 lakhs, 90% placement success, 202 total recruiters.
  - Students receive company-specific training from placement trainers.
  - A Placement Handbook and Placement Brochure are available for download from the university website.
  - Placement drives are conducted throughout the academic year across all campuses.
  - Top recruiters include companies from IT, manufacturing, banking, consulting, healthcare, and education sectors.
Important Information:
  - Placement outcomes depend on student performance, market conditions, and company requirements.
  - The CDC supports students but does not guarantee placement.
  - Contact the Career Development Cell at your campus for specific placement-related queries.
  - Students are encouraged to participate in training programmes from the first year itself.

Overview: CUTM provides separate hostel accommodation for boys and girls across all its campuses. Hostels are equipped with essential amenities including WiFi, 24/7 security, power backup, water supply, and recreational facilities. Each campus has multiple hostel blocks with varying room configurations.
Details:
  - Separate hostels for boys and girls at every campus with dedicated wardens and security.
  - Room types: Shared rooms (2-4 sharing), single rooms (subject to availability), AC rooms, Non-AC rooms.
  - Each room is furnished with bed, study table, chair, cupboard, and fan/AC as applicable.
  - Common facilities: WiFi internet, common rooms with TV, indoor games, reading rooms, water coolers, purifiers, and RO systems.
  - 24/7 security with CCTV surveillance, security guards, and biometric access in select hostels.
  - Power backup for all hostel buildings.
  - Housekeeping and maintenance services provided regularly.
  - Laundry facilities available (self-service or paid service depending on campus).
  - Visitor rooms and guest rooms available for parents/guardians.
  - Medical support: First-aid available; tie-up with nearby hospitals for emergencies.
  - ATM facilities available on or near campus.
Important Information:
  - Hostel allocation is subject to availability — early application is recommended.
  - Hostel fees are separate from academic tuition fees.
  - A refundable caution deposit may be required at the time of check-in.
  - Hostel rules and regulations must be followed; violations may result in disciplinary action.
  - Students must maintain cleanliness and follow the hostel code of conduct.
  - Ragging is strictly prohibited as per university anti-ragging policy.
  - Hostel timings and curfew rules apply; late entry requires prior permission.
  - Visitors are allowed only in designated areas with prior approval from the warden.
  - Overnight guests require prior permission from the warden and hostel office.
  - Room changes are permitted only through the hostel office after the allocation period.

Hostel discipline rules?
Overview: CUTM hostel rules are designed to ensure a safe, disciplined, and conducive living environment for all students. All residents must follow the hostel code of conduct.
Details:
  - Ragging is strictly prohibited as per UGC and university anti-ragging policy. Violators face severe disciplinary action including expulsion.
  - Hostel timings: Students must be in the hostel by the designated curfew time (typically 8:00 PM - 9:00 PM depending on campus).
  - Late entry requires prior written permission from the warden.
  - Overnight absence must be informed to the warden in advance.
  - Visitors are allowed only in designated visitor areas during specified hours.
  - Overnight guests are not permitted without prior warden approval.
  - Consumption of alcohol, tobacco, or any intoxicants is strictly prohibited.
  - Possession of weapons, inflammable materials, or electrical appliances (heaters, irons, etc.) is not allowed without permission.
  - Students are responsible for their room and belongings. Lockers are recommended for valuables.
  - Maintaining cleanliness and hygiene in rooms and common areas is mandatory.
  - Noise should be kept to a minimum, especially during study hours (7:00 PM - 6:00 AM).
  - Damages to hostel property will be recovered from the concerned student(s).
  - Room changes require hostel office approval.
  - Students must carry their hostel ID card at all times.
  - Mess timings must be followed.
  - Parking of vehicles in designated areas only.
Important Information:
  - Rules apply to all residents regardless of gender, course, or campus.
  - Violations may result in warnings, fines, disciplinary action, or expulsion from the hostel.
  - The hostel warden has the authority to take disciplinary action as per university rules.
  - Students can appeal disciplinary decisions to the Chief Warden or Dean of Student Welfare.
  - Regular room inspections are conducted by hostel staff.
  - First-time minor violations typically result in a warning; repeated violations lead to stricter action.
FAQs:
Q: What is the curfew time in hostels?
A: Curfew time is typically 8:00 PM to 9:00 PM depending on the campus. Check with your campus hostel office for exact timings.
Q: Can I have visitors in my room?
A: Visitors are allowed only in designated visitor areas, not in student rooms. Prior approval from the warden is required.

A: Parents can stay in guest rooms with prior booking through the hostel office. They cannot stay in student rooms.
Q: What happens if I break hostel rules?
A: Consequences range from warnings to fines to disciplinary action, depending on the severity and frequency of violations.

A: Electrical appliances like heaters, irons, and cooking devices are not allowed without permission. Furniture is provided by the hostel.

Overview: CUTM provides mess facilities in all hostels and campus canteens offering nutritious and hygienic meals. Separate mess arrangements exist for vegetarian and non-vegetarian students.
Details:
  - Mess facilities are available in all hostel complexes.
  - Meal plans typically include breakfast, lunch, evening snacks, and dinner.
  - Both vegetarian and non-vegetarian meal options are available.
  - Special dietary requirements can be accommodated on request (medical or religious).
  - Mess fees are separate from hostel fees and are typically paid per semester.
  - Menu is planned by the mess committee with student representatives.
  - Campus canteens and food courts are also available for students and staff.
  - Hygienic food preparation and clean dining areas are maintained.
  - Mess timings are displayed in the dining hall.
Important Information:
  - Mess fees are not included in hostel fees — they are charged separately.
  - Students can choose meal plans based on their preferences.
  - Feedback on food quality can be submitted to the mess committee or hostel office.
  - Outside food delivery may be allowed in designated areas.
  - Mess committee includes student representatives for menu planning and quality monitoring.

Q: Is mess fee included in hostel fee?

Q: Is vegetarian food available?

Q: Can I get special diet food?
A: Special dietary requirements for medical or religious reasons can be accommodated on request.

A: The mess committee, which includes student representatives, plans the menu.
